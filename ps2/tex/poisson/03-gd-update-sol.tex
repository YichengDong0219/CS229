\begin{answer}

Based on GLM and the results in a), we have:
\[ p(y|x; \theta) = \frac{1}{y!} \exp(\theta^T x y - e^{\theta^T x}) \]
The derivative of the log likelihood is defined as follows:
\begin{align*}
    \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta_j} \log p(y|x; \theta) &= \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta_j} (\log(\frac{1}{y!})+ (\theta^T x) y - e^{\theta^T x}) \quad (\text{removing irrelevant terms}) \\
    &= \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta_j} \left( y \sum_k \theta_k x_k - e^{\sum_k \theta_k x_k} \right) \\
    &= y x_j - e^{\theta^\top x} x_j \\
    &= x_j \left( y - e^{\theta^\top x} \right)
\end{align*}
    
We want to maximize this log likelihood given some theta - via SGD and the gradient $\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta_j} \log p(y|x;\theta)$ we can nudge on $\theta_j$ in the right direction.

\begin{align*}
    \theta_j &:= \theta_j + \alpha \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta_j} l(\theta) \\
    \theta_j &:= \theta_j + \alpha(y - e^{\theta^T x})x_j
\end{align*}
\end{answer}
